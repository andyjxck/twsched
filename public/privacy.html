Privacy notice for staff

White Swan staff rota (tws-rota.co.uk) · Last updated 26 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information the White Swan staff rota app holds about you, why, who can see it, how long it is kept and what your rights are under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who is responsible for your data

Your employer, The White Swan, High Street, Henley-in-Arden, is the controller of the personal data in this app. Questions or requests about your data can go to a manager or to hello@tws-rota.co.uk.

What we hold and why

InformationWhy we use itLawful basis
Name, work email, phone number, job title, role and login detailsTo give you an account, contact you about shifts and keep the app secureContract; legitimate interests (running the business safely)
Rota, shifts, availability, holiday and time-off requests, shift swaps, hours worked and breaksTo plan the rota, approve leave and record hours for payContract; legal obligation (working time and holiday records)
Pay rate, payroll details, payslips and contract documentsTo pay you correctly and give you your payslipsContract; legal obligation (HMRC payroll records)
Right to work documents (for example a passport page, share code check or visa) and the date they were checkedTo meet the legal duty to check you are allowed to work in the UKLegal obligation
Notifications and a record of key actions (for example who approved a request)To tell you about changes and to keep an accurate recordLegitimate interests

Right to work documents can show your nationality, date of birth and photograph, so they are treated with extra care: only owners and managers who need them can open them.

Who can see your information

  • You can see your own profile, shifts, requests, payslips and documents.
  • Other team members see only colleagues' names and job titles (for example when picking someone to swap a shift with). They cannot see your email, phone number, pay, payslips or documents.
  • Managers can see the rota, staff contact details, requests and right to work status so they can run the team.
  • Owners and anyone given payroll access can see pay information and payslips.
  • The app's developer has a maintenance login so problems can be fixed. It is listed on the owners' Team page, and owners can see that it exists.

Where it is stored

The app runs on Google Firebase. Your data and files are stored in Google's London (europe-west2) data centre. Google acts as our processor under its data processing terms. Emails from the app (for example sign-in links, password resets and rota updates) are sent from noreply@tws-rota.co.uk through an email delivery provider acting as our processor.

How long we keep it

  • Right to work copies: for as long as you work here and for two years after you leave, then they are deleted.
  • Payroll records and payslips: at least three years from the end of the tax year they relate to, and longer where other employment record rules need it.
  • Rota, hours and holiday records: as long as the law requires for working time, minimum wage and holiday records.
  • Your account: closed when you leave. Information we no longer need is deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see a copy of your data, have it corrected, have it deleted or restricted where the law allows, object to how we use it, or receive it in a portable format. Ask a manager or email hello@tws-rota.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Keeping it safe

Access is controlled by your login and your role. Security rules on the server stop people seeing data they are not allowed to, whatever device they use. Please keep your password to yourself and tell a manager straight away if you think someone else has used your account.
